---
title: "CinemaSub"
summary: "Android app that shows subtitles on your phone during a cinema screening, with a start timer, library and subtitle search."
role: "Solo project"
context: "Personal project"
---

CinemaSub began as a small companion for watching films in the cinema in their original language: load a subtitle file onto the phone, start a clock when the film begins, and the subtitles follow along. The display can be dimmed and locked so the screen does not disturb the people around you. The first prototype was a single HTML page and an early Android version called Subtitle App.

Later versions grew into a full library: search, sorting and filters, series grouped into expandable cards, subtitle search through SubDL with TMDB posters and episode titles, a floating subtitle window with a "film starts in" timer, resuming after the app was closed, adjustable subtitle appearance, and backup and restore. When a title matches on TMDB, the cinema view shows the film's proper title instead of the file name. In October 2026 I added a video player for local files, based on Next Player.
