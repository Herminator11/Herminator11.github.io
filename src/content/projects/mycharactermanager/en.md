A dashboard for my own Dungeons & Dragons 5e characters, designed for the phone at the table rather than as a rules database. Changing hit points or using a spell slot is a single tap, and phone and laptop show the same state without reloading.

Characters can be imported from a fillable PDF character sheet (the official D&D sheet or an extended format). The import shows everything it found before anything is saved, and after a level-up the same character can be updated while the current game state, such as hit points and used spell slots, is kept. Everything lives in one SQLite file, and the app can be added to the phone's home screen.
