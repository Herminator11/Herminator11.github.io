---
title: "MyWatchlist"
summary: "Android app to keep track of films and series: want to watch, watching, favourites and history, with search and posters."
role: "Solo project"
context: "Personal project"
---

MyWatchlist is my own place to keep track of what I want to watch, what I am watching and what I have seen. Titles are found through a search backed by TMDB, which also supplies the posters. Series carry a season, which can be asked for right when adding something to the Want to Watch list.

The app has separate views for currently watching, want to watch, favourites and recently watched, a settings screen, and an export of the library. It stores everything on the device.
