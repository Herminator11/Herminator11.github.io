This project is an unofficial digital version of the card game ito, for playing together in one room. The host runs the Android app, which starts a small local web server. Everyone else scans a QR code and joins in the browser on their own phone with a name and an avatar, for two to eight players.

The app guides the group through the round: lobby, a vote on one of three suggested topics with the final topic drawn by a roulette, a hint phase in which each player sees a secret number on a card and gives a clue for it, a presentation, putting the cards in order by dragging, and a reveal in which the cards flip over one by one. At the end the app shows whether the order held and marks the cards that are out of place.

The host switches the whole game between German and English: the host app, the topic pool and every browser client follow the choice. Each language has 120 topics in 12 categories. Players who reload the page stay in the game, and the host's screen stays on while the server runs.
