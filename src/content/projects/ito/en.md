ito is a card game, and this project is a digital version of it for playing together in one room. The host runs the Android app, which starts a small local web server. Everyone else joins from the browser on their own phone with a name and an emoji, for two to eight players.

The app guides the group through the rounds: lobby, a vote on one of three suggested topics, with the final topic drawn by a roulette, a hint phase in which each player sees a secret number on a card and gives a clue for it, a presentation, putting the cards in order, and a reveal in which the cards flip over. Topics are included in English, German and Korean.
